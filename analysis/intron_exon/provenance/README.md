# Numerical execution history

`mesh_check_initial.csv` and `refinement_log.csv` are retained records of
coarse-grid checking and refinement during the original analysis. They are
historical execution logs and are not regenerated by `reproduce_analysis.py`.

Checks of the **final recorded fits** are reproducible with
`python reproduce_analysis.py --stage numerics`, which writes
`fitresults/mesh_refinement.csv` and `fitresults/quadrature_validation.csv`.
Elapsed optimization times are machine dependent and have no scientific role.
